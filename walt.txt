Sunday, WALT Model forecast supports the development of organized thunderstorms capable of producing moderate wind impacts. As storms move through the area, strong downdrafts and outflow boundaries are likely to generate gusts in the 40–60 mph range, which may knock down small branches, move loose outdoor objects, and produce brief bursts of wind-driven rain. The presence of strong surface shear allows storms to organize and maintain structure for a time. However, low-level rotation is unlikely. Updraft strength may briefly support isolated small hail, generally pea- to dime-size, within the strongest storm cores. Overall, the most probable outcome is a period of heavy rain, lightning, and gusty winds as thunderstorms pass through, with localized stronger gusts possible near the leading edge of the storm or under collapsing rain cores.
