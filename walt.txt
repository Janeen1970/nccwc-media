March 24-25, 2026: WALT Model suggests an environment supportive of strong thunderstorms but not a highly organized severe outbreak. Storms that develop in this setup will likely be scattered and somewhat pulse-type, strengthening briefly before weakening as they move through the area. The primary impacts would be gusty winds around 40–60 mph, capable of blowing around loose objects and breaking small tree limbs, along with moderate hail, generally small to near-severe size in the stronger updrafts. Rainfall may become briefly heavy under the storm cores, but storms should move through without prolonged flooding. Because surface shear is weak the environment does not strongly support tornado formation, and the overall event would most likely consist of strong thunderstorms producing bursts of wind, pockets of hail, and brief heavy rain before weakening or moving out of the area.
