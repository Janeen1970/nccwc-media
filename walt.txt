WALT Model AI is an innovative forecasting system developed by Climate Scientist Wayne Adams. The technology is currently copyrighted and filed for patent protection in 2025. It represents a new approach to environmental hazard prediction. The system is capable of producing forecasts at multiple scales, including area-wide, city-level, and street-level analysis. During adverse weather events, the model can provide detailed city-level forecasts to help identify potential impact zones. WALT Model was named Lancaster after a woman and man in Arkansas killed in a tornado at Booneville Arkansas Feb. 20, 1997 with no warning.
