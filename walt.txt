WALT AI Winter Weather Outlook: Winter weather may return to Eastern Oklahoma and Western Arkansas from Wednesday night into Thursday morning. The WALT AI Model highlights icing mainly in elevated terrain and on bridges/overpasses, including ridges and shaded slopes. Valleys remain mostly wet, with slick spots forming first on higher ground, elevated road decks, ramps, and parking lots. The long-range model continues to signal a line of storms ahead of the December 15 cold front, but WALT AI indicates these storms may struggle to sustain intensity. Expect brief gusts along the leading edge (gust front), while surface-based tornado potential remains very low. Late December 15 into the morning of December 16, behind the front, the WALT AI Model shows Snow will likely total around a half inch to 3 inches, wet and sticky, with the best accumulation on grass and elevated surfaces. A brief period of patchy ice is possible during the event on bridges, ramps, and parking lots, followed by slush refreezing later as temperatures fall below 30°F.
