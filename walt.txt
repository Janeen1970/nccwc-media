Friday into Friday night, WALT Model indicates an environment capable of producing organized severe thunderstorms. Storms are likely to develop as discrete or semi-discrete cells initially, supported by sufficient atmospheric energy and shear to allow strong updrafts and rotation to form within individual storms. During this early stage, hail and localized tornado potential can occur, especially if a storm remains isolated long enough to strengthen. As additional storms form and interact, the system will likely transition into a more organized line of thunderstorms, shifting the dominant hazard toward damaging straight-line winds with embedded pockets of rotation along the leading edge. Overall, the event would most likely feature a period of isolated severe storms followed by a fast-moving line capable of strong winds, hail, and brief tornadoes.
