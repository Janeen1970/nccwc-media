Far Eastern Oklahoma and Western Arkansas: WALT Model AI Friday night to Saturday morning supports a solid storm with some punch, but not a full-blown destructive system. You’re likely looking at gusty winds 40–60 mph gusts and some hail 0.75" to 1.25". WALT Model AI is an innovative forecasting system developed by Climate Scientist Wayne Adams. The technology is currently copyrighted and filed for patent protection in 2025. It represents a new approach to environmental hazard prediction. The system is capable of producing forecasts at multiple scales, including area-wide, city-level, and street-level analysis. During adverse weather events, the model can provide detailed city-level forecasts to help identify potential impact zones. WALT Model was partly named Lancaster after a woman, and a man killed in a tornado at Booneville, Arkansas, on Feb. 20, 1997, with no warning.
