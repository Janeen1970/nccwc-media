WALT Model AI is an innovative forecasting system developed by Climate Scientist Wayne Adams. The technology is currently copyrighted and was filed for patent protection in 2025. It introduces a new methodology for environmental hazard forecasting and analysis. The system is designed to generate forecasts across multiple scales, including regional, city-level, and street-level assessments. During severe weather situations, the WALT Model can deliver detailed city-based forecasts to help identify areas with elevated impact potential. The name “WALT” was partially inspired by Lancaster, honoring both a woman and a man who lost their lives in the Booneville, Arkansas, tornado on February 20, 1997, when no warning was issued.
