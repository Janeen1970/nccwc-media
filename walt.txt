WALT Model supports organized severe thunderstorms on Tuesday capable of producing damaging winds. Storms would likely begin as strong individual cells and quickly organize due to the strong surface shear, allowing powerful downdrafts to form. As storms mature, they would likely transition into a line or bowing segment, where the main hazard becomes damaging straight-line winds in the 60–80 mph range. These winds could knock down trees, damage power lines, and cause scattered structural damage. Hail may occur in the strongest cores, and there remains a localized chance of brief rotation or a short-lived tornado, especially early in storm development before the system fully organizes into a line. Overall, the most probable outcome in this scenario is a severe thunderstorm event dominated by damaging wind gusts moving through the area.
