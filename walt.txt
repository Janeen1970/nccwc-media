WALT Model AI is an innovative forecasting system developed by climate scientist Wayne Adams. The technology is currently copyrighted and filed for patent protection in 2025. It represents a new approach to environmental hazard prediction. The system is capable of producing forecasts at multiple scales, including area-wide, city-level, and street-level analysis. During adverse weather events, the model can provide detailed city-level forecasts to help identify potential impact zones. Future upgrades will include optional house-level forecasting services available through a premium feature.
