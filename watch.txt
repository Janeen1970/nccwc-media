  Day 1 Convective Outlook  
   NWS Storm Prediction Center Norman OK
   1257 AM CDT Tue Aug 25 2026

   Valid 251200Z - 261200Z

   ...THERE IS A SLIGHT RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS ACROSS THE CENTRAL
   PLAINS AND UPPER MISSISSIPPI VALLEY...

   ...SUMMARY...
   Scattered severe storms are likely across the central/southern
   Plains this afternoon and evening, and also possible across portions
   of the upper Mississippi Valley. Severe wind gusts and large hail
   are the primary hazards.

   ...Central/southern Plains...
   The region will be influenced by a modest amplification of the
   large-scale pattern with a resultant increase in northwesterly flow
   aloft by late today into tonight, while a front progresses
   southeastward across the central High Plains. The front, as it
   intercepts a moist and moderate to strongly unstable boundary layer,
   will influence increasing thunderstorm development by mid/late
   afternoon, with an orographically influenced increase in
   thunderstorms also occurring along the Raton Mesa/Front Range.

   Influenced by the strengthening northwesterlies aloft, effective
   shear across Nebraska and western Kansas will generally at least be
   35-40 kt. On the eastern periphery of a stout elevated mixed layer,
   this will support some initial supercells capable of large hail,
   most probable across western/southern Nebraska into
   northern/possibly western Kansas.

   Influenced by the front and aggregating outflow, one or more
   southeastward-moving clusters capable of wind damage will likely
   evolve by evening across southern Nebraska and Kansas. While winds
   aloft will not be as strong, a similar upscale-growing quasi-linear
   trend will probably also occur with high-based storms from the Raton
   Mesa vicinity into the Oklahoma/Texas Panhandles, and perhaps nearby
   Oklahoma, with severe-caliber wind gust potential spreading
   east-southeastward through the evening.

   ...Eastern Minnesota/western Wisconsin/western upper Michigan...
   A shortwave trough (and related mid/high-level speed max) will dig
   southeastward over southern Manitoba toward northern Minnesota and
   Lake Superior tonight. While residual early day convection and cloud
   cover may curb destabilization for a time, and keep the warm sector
   somewhat narrow, strong to severe thunderstorm development should
   occur near the eastward-advancing front by around mid/late
   afternoon. 60s F dewpoints will contribute to a zone of 2000+ J/kg
   MLCAPE across the eastern half of Minnesota/far northwest Wisconsin
   by peak heating. Some supercells will be possible aside from more
   prevalent multicells/small clusters. Large hail and damaging winds
   are expected to be the primary hazards, at least on a widely
   scattered basis.

   ...Coastal Southeast/middle Gulf Coast...
   A moist/unstable environment will be favorable for isolated
   pulse-type severe thunderstorms capable of water-loaded downdrafts
   and wind damage, particularly during mid/late afternoon through
   around sunset. This currently appears most probable across coastal
   South Carolina, southern Georgia and northern Florida, although some
   strong thunderstorm wind potential might also exist across the
   middle Gulf Coast such as southern Louisiana.

   ..Guyer/Weinman.. 08/25/2026
