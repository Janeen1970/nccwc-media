  Day 1 Convective Outlook  
   NWS Storm Prediction Center Norman OK
   1258 AM CDT Thu Aug 13 2026

   Valid 131200Z - 141200Z

   ...THERE IS A SLIGHT RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS ACROSS PARTS OF
   IOWA INTO ILLINOIS...

   ...SUMMARY...
   Isolated to scattered severe thunderstorms appear possible today
   from parts of the southern/central High Plains to the mid
   Mississippi and lower Ohio Valleys. Damaging winds should be the
   main threat, but occasional hail may also occur.

   ...Iowa into Illinois/Indiana...
   An MCV has developed early this morning with an ongoing cluster of
   thunderstorms across central NE. This activity is expected to spread
   eastward through the morning, and should be in the vicinity of
   western/central IA by the start of the period. There is still
   uncertainty regarding how intense this cluster will be due to the
   presence of fairly substantial MLCIN downstream into IA. Even so,
   warm/moist advection associated with a low-level jet over the
   southern/central Plains and the presence of a rather moist/unstable
   airmass into IA suggest some potential for an organized cluster to
   pose a risk for scattered severe/damaging winds as it moves eastward
   through the morning. A brief embedded tornado may also occur if
   convection can remain surface based.

   Even greater uncertainty exists this afternoon regarding potential
   convective evolution, with most guidance showing the cluster
   weakening as it moves into IN. Regardless, the airmass downstream
   and to the south of outflow associated with the morning cluster will
   destabilize through the day, and any additional thunderstorms that
   form could pose at least an isolated threat for damaging winds and
   perhaps some hail given the strong buoyancy forecast.

   ...Southern/Central High Plains...
   High-based thunderstorms are forecast to initially develop over the
   higher terrain of the central Rockies/Front Range this afternoon,
   with post-frontal low-level upslope flow persisting across the
   central High Plains. As this activity spreads east-northeastward and
   encounters greater instability, consolidating outflows should
   support some increase for isolated severe gusts given steepened
   low-level lapse rates. Occasional hail may also occur with the
   stronger cores that develop. Consideration was given to including
   greater severe wind probabilities across eastern CO into parts of
   KS, but modest deep-layer shear casts some doubt on overall
   thunderstorm organization/intensity. A somewhat separate area of
   thunderstorms may also develop farther south across the southern
   High Plains in a very hot and well-mixed boundary layer. This
   activity should also pose a threat for isolated severe gusts through
   the afternoon.

   ...Great Basin...
   Modestly enhanced southwesterly mid-level flow will persist today
   across the Great Basin downstream of a weak mid-level trough over
   CA. Weak to locally moderate instability is forecast to develop
   through the afternoon as filtered diurnal heating occurs. Scattered
   to numerous thunderstorms are expected to form and spread
   northeastward across this destabilizing airmass, with strong to
   severe gusts possible. Based on latest guidance trends (00Z HREF),
   have expanded the Marginal Risk for severe/damaging winds to include
   more of the Great Basin.

   ...Northeast...
   Seasonably cool mid-level temperatures are forecast today across
   parts of the Northeast, with a mid-level trough expected to move
   east-southeastward from Quebec into New England. While instability
   is forecast to remain fairly limited, strong west-northwesterly flow
   aloft may support a risk for isolated damaging winds with
   thunderstorms that develop and spread southeastward through the
   afternoon. Isolated hail may also occur with the stronger cells.
