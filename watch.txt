   ...SUMMARY...
   Thunderstorms with isolated severe wind gusts and hail will be
   possible over parts of the Intermountain West this afternoon and
   evening. A few strong to severe gusts may also occur across portions
   of the central High Plains.

   ...ID/UT...
   Morning water vapor imagery shows an upper low over northern CA,
   with a band of strong flow aloft with associated embedded shortwave
   troughs extending across NV into UT/ID.  Strong heating across this
   area will aid in the development of scattered thunderstorms from
   southern ID into northern UT by mid-afternoon.  Sufficient vertical
   shear is present for rotating updrafts capable of large hail and
   gusty/damaging winds.  While coverage of storms will likely be
   limited, one or two severe cells are expected later today - mainly
   over the eastern Snake River Plain.

   ...CO/KS/NE...
   The remnants of a weak tropical low are tracking northeastward
   across the four-corners region this morning.  Ample moisture and
   weak ascent associated with this feature will lead to considerable
   clouds and scattered thunderstorms today over the mountains of CO
   and southern WY.  This activity will spread eastward into eastern CO
   and western NE/KS with high-based convection posing a risk of
   locally damaging wind gusts.  The extent of this severe threat will
   be modulated by daytime heating, which is uncertain at this time due
   to lingering cloud cover.
