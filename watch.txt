  Day 1 Convective Outlook  
   NWS Storm Prediction Center Norman OK
   0714 AM CDT Sun Aug 30 2026

   Valid 301300Z - 311200Z

   ...THERE IS A SLIGHT RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS THIS AFTERNOON AND
   EVENING OVER PARTS OF THE DAKOTAS...

   ...SUMMARY...
   Severe storms capable of damaging wind gusts are possible across
   portions of the northern Plains this afternoon and evening.

   ...MT/ND/SD...
   Morning water vapor loop shows a strong upper trough over ID
   tracking east-northeastward.  This system will emerge onto the
   Plains of eastern MT/western Dakotas by late afternoon today, when
   strong heating will result in a corridor of surface temperatures
   near 90F along the MT/ND border ahead of an approaching cold front. 
   Thunderstorms will intensify in vicinity of this front through the
   afternoon, tracking quickly eastward into parts of western/central
   ND/SD by 00z.  Morning CAM solutions suggest storms will organize
   into a line with the risk of severe wind gusts.  Given the strength
   of the low and mid-level flow near the front, that scenario seems
   plausible.  Therefore have added a SLGT risk to parts of this
   region.

   ...CO/KS/NE...
   South of the primary severe threat, a secondary area of concern will
   develop this afternoon as storms develop over the mountains of
   central CO and spread eastward into the Plains.  This activity will
   be high-based and in a weakly unstable environment.  Nevertheless,
   strong outflow winds will be possible with the activity, spreading
   eastward into western NE and northwest KS during the evening.

   ...UT/Western CO...
   Considerable low-level moisture will remain over UT today, leading
   to another round of strong thunderstorms this afternoon.  Deep-layer
   shear remains sufficient for rotating storms capable of damaging
   wind gusts and hail.  Activity should spread into western CO by
   early evening before weakening.
