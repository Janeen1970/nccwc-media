   ...THERE IS A SLIGHT RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS THIS AFTERNOON
   OVER PARTS OF THE NORTHEAST STATES...AND THIS EVENING OVER THE
   CENTRAL/NORTHERN PLAINS AND UPPER MIDWEST....

   ...SUMMARY...
   Scattered strong to severe storms are possible over the central
   Plains and Midwest, as well as parts of New York, Pennsylvania and
   New Jersey.

   ...Northeast...
   A broad upper ridge remains in place over the Mid MS/TN Valleys,
   with northwest flow aloft affecting the northeast US and
   Mid-Atlantic states.  The main baroclinic zone in this region
   currently extends from central NY into southern New England, with
   dewpoints in the mid-upper 60s along and south of the boundary. This
   air mass is expected to destabilize through the day as temperatures
   warm through the 80s, leading to a zone of strong instability
   (MLCAPE of 2500+ J/kg) over parts of PA/NY/NJ.  Ongoing convection
   may delay daytime heating, but eventually strong storms are expected
   to form in this region, spreading southeastward through the late
   afternoon and evening.  Damaging winds are the primary concern,
   although some hail and a tornado or two cannot be ruled out.  A lack
   of a more obvious upper feature to aid in large-scale forcing may
   limit the overall coverage of severe storms.

   ...SD/NE/MN/IA...
   A shortwave trough currently over western CO will track quickly
   northeastward and affect the central Plains by late afternoon. 
   Strong heating and a moist air mass will yield moderate CAPE over
   parts of SD/NE.  Low-level warm advection and diurnal heating will
   lead to scattered thunderstorm development over central SD by late
   afternoon.  Initial supercell storms are expected, capable of
   damaging winds, large hail, and perhaps a tornado.  Storms are
   expected to become more numerous during the evening and grow upscale
   into bowing structures, with a damaging wind risk tracking eastward
   into parts of southern MN and perhaps northern IA.  Some guidance
   suggests storms will maintain some damaging wind risk into central
   WI overnight.

   ...Southwest LA/Southeast TX...
   Tropical Storm Eduardo is expected to strengthen today off the
   immediate LA/TX coast and move inland this afternoon and evening. 
   Models continue to show limited discrete convection in the northeast
   quadrant of the system, suggested a rather low tornado risk today. 
   Nevertheless, given the potential for rapid strengthening around the
   time of landfall, have opted for a small 2% tornado risk area for a
   few hours this evening.
