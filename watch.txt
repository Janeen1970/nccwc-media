   0730 AM CDT Sun Sep 27 2026

   ...THERE IS A MARGINAL RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS FOR PORTIONS OF
   THE CENTRAL AND NORTHERN HIGH PLAINS...

   ...SUMMARY...
   Isolated thunderstorms capable of large hail will be possible across
   portions of western Nebraska and the Black Hills of South Dakota.

   ...Portions of the Central and Northern High Plains ...
   A residual moist axis over the Plains will feature 60s deg F surface
   dewpoints extending from the southern Plains northward into NE/SD. 
   The western periphery of the moist plume will undergo strong heating
   despite some increasing high cloud cover.  Forcing will likely
   remain negligible through the day and limit storm coverage with
   moderate buoyancy developing in western NE.  During the evening,
   thunderstorm coverage may increase in response to increasing
   large-scale ascent associated with an approaching subtle vorticity
   maxima and an increase in the low-level jet.  Large hail will be
   possible with the strongest of these storms.

   Elsewhere, scattered thunderstorms are forecast over north and
   central TX late this afternoon, as well as isolated to widely
   scattered storms over northern UT into central WY.  Localized gusty
   winds are possible with the stronger storms, but uncertainty in
   expected coverage of these gusts and magnitude of the potential
   threat preclude a low-probability severe highlight this outlook
   update.
