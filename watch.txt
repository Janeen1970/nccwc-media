   NWS Storm Prediction Center Norman OK
   1128 AM CDT Mon Sep 28 2026

   Valid 281630Z - 291200Z

   ...THERE IS A MARGINAL RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS ACROSS PARTS OF
   THE NORTHERN PLAINS...

   ...SUMMARY...
   Isolated strong to severe thunderstorms may occur this afternoon and
   evening across parts of western/northern South Dakota and vicinity.

   ...Northern Plains...
   Clouds and elevated thunderstorms remain prevalent late this morning
   across parts of the Dakotas. A narrow window for strong to locally
   severe thunderstorms may exist this afternoon/evening across parts
   of western/northern SD and vicinity as convection develops
   along/east of a weak surface trough. While lapse rates aloft should
   remain modest, filtered daytime heating with surface temperatures
   reaching the mid/upper 70s will probably be sufficient for robust
   thunderstorm development. A belt of modestly enhanced southwesterly
   mid-level flow will extend across the northern Rockies/Plains today,
   and 30-40 kt of deep-layer shear should support updraft
   organization. Current expectations are for thunderstorms to
   initially form near the Black Hills by 20-22Z, and then spread
   east-northeastward along/near a surface front through the evening.
   The stronger cells may be capable of producing isolated hail and
   gusty winds, before convection tends to become elevated with
   eastward extent across northern SD/southern ND by late evening.

   ...Lower Colorado River Valley...
   Showers and thunderstorms should develop this afternoon as an
   anomalously moist low-level airmass spreads northward ahead of a
   mid-level shortwave trough forecast to move from CA/NV to the lower
   CO River Valley through the period. Cloudiness and weak mid-level
   lapse rates should limit instability through early afternoon, before
   cloud cover gradually diminishes through early evening. A stronger
   thunderstorm or two may occur parts of interior southern
   CA/northwest AZ/southern NV, but the overall risk for severe
   hail/wind appears too limited to include any probabilities for this
   region at this time.
