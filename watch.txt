 Day 1 Convective Outlook  
   NWS Storm Prediction Center Norman OK
   0800 PM CDT Sun Sep 20 2026

   Valid 210100Z - 211200Z

   ...THERE IS A SLIGHT RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS ACROSS PARTS OF
   THE CENTRAL HIGH PLAINS....

   ...SUMMARY...
   Thunderstorms with large hail and severe wind gusts will be possible
   this evening across parts of the central High Plains. A few severe
   wind gusts may also occur from the Ozarks northeastward into the
   Ohio Valley.

   ...Central High Plains...
   The latest water vapor imagery shows a shortwave mid-level trough
   over eastern Colorado. An axis of moderate instability, with MLCAPE
   in the 1500 to 2000 J/kg range, is analyzed by the RAP just ahead of
   this feature from near the Colorado-Kansas state line southeastward
   into the Oklahoma Panhandle. An isolated supercell is currently
   ongoing near the instability axis near Lamar, Colorado. RAP forecast
   soundings ahead of this cell across far southwest Kansas early this
   evening have MLCAPE in the 1500 to 2000 J/kg range, 0-6 km shear
   near 40 knots and 700-500 mb lapse rates around 7 C/km. This
   environment will continue to support supercells capable of large
   hail and wind damage. The threat could persist into the mid to late
   evening with parts of southwest Kansas and the Oklahoma Panhandle
   being affected. Later this evening, short-term model forecasts also
   suggest that redevelopment may occur to the east of the Denver
   Metro, with a new cluster potentially tracking southeastward near
   the instability axis. This activity could also pose a large hail and
   wind damage threat.

   ...Ozarks into the Ohio Valley...
   Several minor perturbations, embedded in westerly flow aloft, appear
   to moving through a corridor from the Ozarks eastward into the Ohio
   Valley. The most pronounced feature appears to be located in
   southeastern Indiana, ahead of which a small cluster of severe
   storms is ongoing. Along the projected track of these storms, the
   RAP has MLCAPE in the 1200 to 1500 J/kg range with 0-6 km around 35
   knots. This should continue to support an isolated wind-damage
   threat for a couple more hours early this evening...see MCD 2319.

   Further southwest into the parts of the Ozarks and mid Mississippi
   Valley, moderate instability is analyzed by the RAP from far
   northeast Oklahoma northeastward into south-central Illinois.
   Isolated to scattered thunderstorms are ongoing near the instability
   axis. These storms may be capable of producing isolated severe wind
   gusts for another hour or two this evening...see MCD 2318.
