   ...DISCUSSION...
   For Wed/D4 through about Fri/D6, severe chances will be minimal
   across the CONUS as a broad upper trough affects the Great Lakes
   into the Northeast, with high pressure keeping the higher dewpoints
   primarily offshore over the Gulf of America.

   For late Fri/D6 into Sun/D8, predictability becomes low as models
   indicate a possible tropical cyclone over parts of the southwestern
   Gulf of America (NHC 30% change of cyclone formation). This, along
   with a possible weak shortwave trough developing over TX/northern
   Gulf Coast, could result in a rapid northward surge of tropical
   moisture and enhanced shear. This area will be monitored in upcoming
   outlooks as the situation evolves.
