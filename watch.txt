Day 1 Convective Outlook  
   NWS Storm Prediction Center Norman OK
   1109 AM CDT Fri Sep 25 2026

   Valid 251630Z - 261200Z

   ...NO SEVERE THUNDERSTORM AREAS FORECAST...

   ...SUMMARY...
   Organized severe thunderstorms are not expected today.

   ...Synopsis...

   An MCS is decaying across the TX Panhandle/South Plains late this
   morning. Outflow associated with this area of convection is
   spreading south and east, while areas of rain and cloud cover slowly
   develop northeastward toward western OK. In the wake of this early
   day convection, some airmass recovery and modest destabilization is
   possible across southeast NM into far southwest TX where stronger
   heating is occurring amid more scattered cloudiness. Likewise,
   across western OK ahead of the TX Panhandle convection and outflow,
   more isolated cloud cover is allowing for stronger heating of a
   moist boundary layer. 

   ...Southeast NM/Southwest TX...

   Midlevel lapse rates are expected to remain modest and MLCAPE will
   range from 500-1000 J/kg by mid/late afternoon. Isolated convection
   may develop near higher terrain initially across southeast
   NM/southwest TX. A storm or two could briefly become strong and
   produce marginal hail, but the severe risk is expected to be
   tempered by the marginal thermodynamic/kinematic parameter space in
   the wake of the early day MCS. 

   ...Western OK...

   While currently mostly sunny across western OK, afternoon heating
   could become muted by cloud cover as showers associated with the
   ongoing TX Panhandle activity spread east/northeast the next few
   hours. This could limit stronger destabilization/steepening of
   low-level lapse rates within a modestly sheared environment.  A
   strong gust or two could occur if sufficient heating/steepening of
   low-level lapse rates unfolds. However, given modest vertical shear,
   weak midlevel lapse rates, and marginal instability, the overall
   severe risk appears limited.
