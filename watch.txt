Day 1 Convective Outlook  
   NWS Storm Prediction Center Norman OK
   1249 AM CDT Thu Jul 30 2026

   Valid 301200Z - 311200Z

   ...THERE IS A SLIGHT RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS ACROSS PARTS OF
   THE CENTRAL AND NORTHERN PLAINS...

   ...SUMMARY...
   Thunderstorms with severe wind gusts will be possible this afternoon
   and evening across parts of the central and northern Plains.
   Isolated severe gusts may also occur along parts of the eastern Gulf
   Coast.

   ...Central and Northern Plains...
   At mid-levels, a shortwave ridge will move eastward into the upper
   Mississippi Valley today as a shortwave trough and an associated
   cold front, move through the northern Plains. Ahead of the front,
   surface dewpoints will be in the lower 70s F with moderate
   instability developing across much of the moist airmass by late
   morning. Convection is expected to initiate near the front around
   midday with coverage expanding during the afternoon. MCS development
   will be possible during the late afternoon and early evening from
   eastern South Dakota southwestward into central Nebraska.

   RAP forecast soundings at 21Z from Huron, South Dakota southward to
   near Grand Island, Nebraska have MLCAPE in the 1500 to 2000 J/kg
   range and 0-6 km shear in the 25 to 35 knot range. This should be
   sufficient for a mixed mode including potential for supercells and
   severe line segments. The primary threat will be for damaging wind
   gusts with any cell or line that can become intense in areas that
   destabilize the most. The severe threat may persist into the early
   to mid evening as a relatively large convective cluster moves
   southward into the central Plains.

   Further west into parts of the High Plains, pockets of moderate
   instability may develop from southeastern Colorado into western
   Kansas and across eastern Montana. Although large-scale ascent is
   forecast to be relatively weak in these areas, topographic forcing
   will aid convective initiation. In the mid to late afternoon,
   isolated to scattered storms will move southeastward into the lower
   elevations. Localized strong to severe wind gusts and hail will be
   the primary threats.

   ...Eastern Gulf Coast...
   A quasi-stationary front will be in place today from the Florida
   Panhandle into southern Georgia. Near and to the south of the front,
   surface dewpoints in the 70s F will contribute to moderate
   destabilization. As low-level convergence increases during the day
   near the front, isolated to scattered storms are expected to form
   and move southeastward into the moist and unstable air. Moderate
   instability and steep low-level lapse rates may support an isolated
   threat for severe wind gusts this afternoon.
