 Day 1 Convective Outlook  
   NWS Storm Prediction Center Norman OK
   1100 AM CDT Mon Aug 10 2026

   Valid 101630Z - 111200Z

   ...THERE IS A SLIGHT RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS FROM PARTS OF
   ILLINOIS TO THE MID-ATLANTIC COAST...OVER PARTS OF THE NORTHERN AND
   CENTRAL PLAINS...AND OVER SOUTHERN ARIZONA...

   ...SUMMARY...
   Thunderstorms with severe wind gusts will be possible this afternoon
   and evening from northern Illinois eastward into Pennsylvania.
   Severe gusts and hail will also be possible from parts of the
   central and northern Plains into the Midwest, with scattered severe
   storms over southern Arizona.

   ...PA/NY/NJ...
   Multiple clusters and lines of thunderstorms are ongoing this
   morning from western PA.  So far, this activity has produced limited
   severe wind gusts due to weak low-level lapse rates.  However,
   continued heating over central/eastern PA will eventually allow the
   storms to be more efficient wind-producers with locally damaging
   gusts expected.  See MD #1894 for further details.

   ...OH/WV...
   A secondary cluster of storms over central OH is tracking
   southeastward in a moderately sheared environment.  Rapid heating is
   occurring to the southwest of this cluster, which should result in
   intensification by mid afternoon.  Storms will likely spread into
   northern WV with a risk of damaging wind gusts and perhaps hail.

   ...SD/MN/IA...
   A large reservoir of unstable air will reside across much of SD
   today, with MLCAPE values over 3000 J/kg, steep lapse rates, and
   dewpoints in the upper 60s.  A capping inversion should preclude
   sustained convection until after 00z when strengthening southerly
   low-level flow and warm advection will aid initiation.  Supercells
   capable of large hail and damaging winds are expected, with eventual
   upscale organization into a bowing MCS possible through the
   evening/night.

   ...CO/KS...
   Most model guidance shows the likelihood of scattered high-based
   thunderstorm development late this afternoon over northeast CO,
   spreading into northwest KS and southwest NE this evening.  Cloud
   cover is somewhat limiting heating, but sufficient CAPE and winds
   aloft may promote a period of damaging wind potential later today.

   ...AZ...
   Another round of evening thunderstorms is expected to initiate off
   the higher terrain of east-central AZ and spread westward toward the
   TUS/PHX metro areas.  Parameters remain conditionally favorable for
   damaging winds in any sustained/organized clusters of storms that
   may develop.

   ..Hart/Weinman.. 08/10/2026
