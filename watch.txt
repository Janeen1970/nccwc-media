   ...THERE IS A SLIGHT RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS FROM THE
   NORTHERN/CENTRAL PLAINS TO THE LOWER OHIO VALLEY...

   ...SUMMARY...
   Scattered severe thunderstorms are possible today through this
   evening from parts of the mid Mississippi Valley and lower Ohio
   Valley into portions of the central and northern High Plains. 
   Severe gusts and large hail are the primary hazards.

   ...IA to OH Valley...
   Radar/satellite imagery this morning show several thunderstorm
   clusters ongoing from IA into the OH Valley.  The most notable
   thunderstorm cluster is over far eastern IA and is moving into
   central and northern IL this morning.  The airmass downstream of
   this activity is very moist and extremely unstable [reference the 12
   UTC Davenport, IA raob (4300 J/kg MLCAPE)], and it is located near
   the terminus of a 35-kt west-southwesterly LLJ over northern MO. 
   Expecting a gradual intensification of storms this morning across IL
   with a risk for scattered severe gusts and associated wind damage. 
   Considerable uncertainty remains regarding the eventual evolution of
   additional storm clusters over parts of the Midwest through this
   afternoon.  Outflow from morning showers/storms over eastern IN into
   central KY will likely inhibit appreciable severe risk farther east
   across parts of the OH Valley.  Nonetheless, a moist and
   destabilizing boundary layer will lend a risk for scattered wind
   damage and isolated large hail with the stronger storms through the
   late afternoon and evening.  

   ...Northern/Central Plains...
   East/southeasterly low-level flow will maintain modest moisture
   across the Dakotas, increasing with southward extent across NE into
   northern KS.  Near-60 F dewpoints will likely spread westward into
   eastern CO/WY/MT beneath steep lapse rates.  Ample heating will
   result in moderate buoyancy and 30+ kt effective shear---supporting
   some organization to the scattered thunderstorms forecast to develop
   later this afternoon/evening.  Initial cells will pose a threat for
   large hail and severe gusts.  Some clustering may occur during the
   evening but confidence remains low regarding specific corridors
   where the severe threat will focus.  

   ...Lower MI into NY/PA/NJ...
   A belt of stronger northwesterly flow aloft will extend from Lower
   MI into NY/PA today.  A seasonally moist/unstable airmass will be in
   place.  Vertically veering wind profiles will foster 35+ kt
   effective shear magnitudes, while hodographs are fairly elongated.
   Midlevel lapse rates will remain modest, with MLCAPE around
   1000-1500 J/kg expected.  Isolated to widely scattered storms will
   pose a risk for damaging gusts and marginally severe hail.

   ...TN Valley/Southeast...
   Thunderstorms are expected to develop by midday within a very moist
   and strongly unstable airmass.  Deep-layer northwesterly flow will
   remain weak, but steepened 0-2 km lapse rates and high PW (near 2
   inches) will support an isolated risk for some stronger water-loaded
   downdrafts capable of wind damage.

   ...Lower CO River Valley...
   Model guidance shows widely scattered storms developing this
   afternoon near a weak upper trough analyzed this morning via
   water-vapor imagery.  Some of the stronger thunderstorm downdrafts
   may be capable of isolated severe gusts.
