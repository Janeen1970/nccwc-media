   ...THERE IS A SLIGHT RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS FROM EASTERN
   NEVADA INTO NORTHERN UTAH...

   ...SUMMARY...
   Widely scattered strong to severe storms are expected across parts
   of the northern Great Basin, northern/central Rockies, and northern
   Plains. Severe wind gusts are the main threat.

   ...Great Basin into Central/Northern Rockies...
   A broad upper trough is present today across the Southern Rockies
   into OK/TX, with relatively fast flow aloft extending from NV into
   WY.  IR satellite imagery suggests considerable cloud cover is
   affecting much of the Great Basin region, which will limit daytime
   heating/destabilization and overall severe threat.  Nevertheless,
   there appears to be a corridor from eastern NV across parts of
   north-central UT where sufficient heating will result in steep
   low-level lapse rates and favorable CAPE profiles for a few
   high-based thunderstorms capable of damaging wind gusts.  Supercell
   structures are also possible in this corridor, suggesting a risk of
   hail in the strongest storms.

   ...Northern Plains...
   Similar to yesterday, clusters of high-based showers and
   thunderstorms will spread eastward across WY into parts of the
   Dakotas/NE this afternoon and evening.  Favorable winds aloft,
   combined with at least marginal CAPE will aid in a risk of locally
   damaging wind gusts and hail.
