   ...SUMMARY...
   A couple of marginally severe thunderstorms are possible late this
   afternoon into the early evening across parts of the southern High
   Plains.

   ...TX Panhandle into northwest OK and southwest KS...
   An extensive plume of mid to upper level moisture currently extends
   from southern High Plains through central Plains into the eastern
   Dakotas and MN, evidenced by widespread cloudiness and embedded
   showers and isolated thunderstorms. This plume is supported by
   moderate southwesterly flow aloft between the upper ridging centered
   over the Middle/Upper TX coast and lower heights associated with an
   amplifying upper trough approaching the coastal Pacific Northwest. 

   Recent surface analysis shows a corridor of greater low-level
   moisture (characterized by mid to upper 60s dewpoints) extending
   from the TX Low Rolling Plains through the eastern TX and OK
   Panhandles into far southwest KS. Much of this corridor of greater
   low-level moisture is just east of the cloudiness (and cooler
   surface temperatures) associated with the mid/upper moisture plume.
   This region should continue to heat, with afternoon temperatures
   reaching the mid to upper 80s. Lapse rates are poor, but the warm
   temperatures and ample low-level moisture will still support MLCAPE
   from 1500 to 2000 J/kg. This buoyancy combined with low-level
   convergence (either on outflow from the ongoing showers or as a
   result of differential heating) will support isolated to scattered
   thunderstorms this afternoon and evening. Vertical shear will be
   weak across much of the region, and a largely disorganized storm
   mode is anticipated. However, a few water-loaded downbursts are
   possible with the strongest cells. 

   Slightly stronger large-scale forcing for ascent and vertical shear
   is expected into KS as a weak convectively augmented vorticity
   maximum moves trough. Isolated to widely scattered thunderstorms are
   possible here as well, although weaker buoyancy should limit overall
   intensity. Even so, a couple of weak supercells capable of isolated
   hail and/or damaging gusts may occur.
