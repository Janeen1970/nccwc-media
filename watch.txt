   ...THERE IS A SLIGHT RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS ACROSS PARTS OF
   CENTRAL NORTH DAKOTA AND SOUTHEAST SOUTH DAKOTA...

   ...SUMMARY...
   Widely scattered severe storms capable of mainly large hail are
   expected over portions of North Dakota as well as southeast South
   Dakota into northeast Nebraska this afternoon into evening. Isolated
   damaging wind potential will exist over the northern and central
   Plains into the upper Mississippi Valley. Locally damaging wind
   gusts remain possible this afternoon over the central Gulf Coast
   states.

   ...20Z Update...

   north/south-oriented line segment is tracking eastward into an
   increasingly warm PBL. This activity will continue to pose a risk of
   a few severe wind gusts this afternoon. See MCD #2264 for more

   .PREV DISCUSSION... /ISSUED 1112 AM CDT Mon Sep 07 2026/

   ...ND...
   A fast-moving shortwave trough currently over WY/MT will track
   northeastward into the Dakotas this afternoon.  Strong
   heating/mixing in southwest ND will lead to a sharpening dryline
   across central ND by mid/late afternoon.  This will be the likely
   focus for thunderstorm development after 22z.  Forecast soundings in
   the warm-sector east of the dryline suggest sufficient low-level and
   deep-layer shear for supercell storm-modes. Large hail and perhaps a
   tornado or two are possible in the most intense storms. This
   activity will spread eastward into northeast ND this evening before
   weakening.

   ...CO/KS/NE/SD...
   A weak surface boundary is present today from northeast CO into
   central NE.  Scattered afternoon thunderstorms will form over the
   mountains of central CO, with development spreading eastward along
   the boundary through the afternoon.  Strong heating and a deeply
   mixed boundary-layer will promote intense downdrafts in the
   strongest cores.  By early evening, activity may spread into
   southeast SD where greater low-level moisture and CAPE will pose a
   risk of a few more intense storms capable of hail or even a tornado.
    Recent model solutions have been backing off on this SD scenario,
   but will maintain the ongoing SLGT for now.

   ...LA/MS/AL...
   Hot and humid conditions will prevail over the central Gulf coast
   region today, with full sunshine and dewpoints in the low-mid 70s. 
   Steering flow is weak, but high CAPE and ample moisture suggest a
   risk of occasional intense downdrafts in the strongest storms.
